This is the blog of 拉康的a — “Lacan’s *a*”.

The two characters in the footer, 徒然, come from Yoshida Kenkō’s *Tsurezuregusa* (*Essays in Idleness*): with nothing in particular to do, I write down whatever drifts through my mind. In Chinese the same word means “in vain”. Knowing it may all be in vain, I write anyway.

> Countless small thoughts fill a life.
